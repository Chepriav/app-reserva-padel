- `bloqueo` -> `blockout` or `block`
- `bloques` -> `blocks` or `slots`
- `clase` -> `class` or `lesson`

- `horarios` -> `schedules` or `timeSlots`
- `partida` -> `match` or `game`
- `partidas` -> `matches` or `games`
- `semana` -> `week` or `weekday`
